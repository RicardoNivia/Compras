# Módulo Bienestar y Fundación

Detalle movido desde `CLAUDE.md` (sin cambios de contenido). Las convenciones generales siguen en `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Bienestar: actividades y cronograma (`lib/actividadesBienestar.js`, `app/api/actividades/*`, `frontend/components/bienestar/*`, page `/bienestar`)**: `ActividadBienestar` (table `actividades_bienestar`, Int id) is one activity of the yearly plan: nombre, fecha (YYYY-MM-DD string like Record dates), línea (Bienestar/Fundación), estado — lifecycle **creada → programada → ejecutada → cerrada** (only creada may lack a date; cerrada receives no new purchases) —, presupuesto asignado, inscritos/asistentes, responsable and creator. A purchase is associated from the Registros form (create or edit): `Record.actividadId` (real FK, ON DELETE RESTRICT — an activity with purchases cannot be deleted) + `Record.actividad` name snapshot, resolved to the current name by `hydrateConDatosActuales` (same catalog pattern as proveedor); the activity's spend is the sum of `valorConIva` of its purchases. The dashboards' main cards have the same "Explicar" button as Compras (`components/KpiExplain.jsx`, `POST /api/actividades/explicar`: figures come from the server, never the client). Each view is its own page in the Bienestar menu (`/bienestar`, `/bienestar/actividades`, `/bienestar/cronograma`, all through `components/bienestar/BienestarPagina.jsx`): **Resumen** (dashboard from `GET /api/actividades/resumen`: KPIs tagged by source Bienestar/Compras/Cruce, monthly spend of associated purchases vs. attendees, deterministic "Lectura del año", cost per attendee, spend by category, upcoming activities), **Actividades** (paginated table, filters) and **Cronograma** (yearly 12-month view and monthly calendar with Colombian holidays, `+` per day to create). **An activity has ONE page, its ficha (`/bienestar[/fundacion]/actividades/[id]`, `FichaActividad.jsx`), where it is both seen and configured** — there is no separate edit page any more (`/[id]/editar` only redirects to the ficha with `?tab=datos`, or `?tab=compras` for the old `?paso=compras`). The "Formulario de inscripción" section is hidden since November 2026 (commented out in the ficha menu; `TabFormulario`, the public route `/inscripcion/<token>` and the backend still exist). Layout (classes `fx-*`): a header with the activity life cycle (creada → programada → ejecutada → cerrada) and four meters (presupuesto, compras, asistencia, calificación) that jump to their section, then a sticky side menu with the sections grouped by topic (La actividad · Dinero · Personas · Registro · Cierre · Ayuda de Nova) and the content on the right; inside it every field is sized to its data (a value, a date or a number never spans the full width). Its sections put what is configured next to what is shown: Datos de la actividad (name, línea, state, dates, responsable, delete) · Presupuesto (assigned budget + executed / in process / available and cost per attendee) · Compras (amounts at a glance, associated purchases with Quitar, "Asociar compras" — a side panel (`AsociarComprasPanel.jsx`) to search, filter (near the date / área Bienestar) and tick several purchases at once, showing the total and how the budget would end before confirming —, and Solicitudes a Compras last) · Participantes y asistencia (the "aplica participantes" switch, manual totals only while there is no list, the list and its single import). The editable parts are the same `ActividadFormulario` embedded by sections (`embebido` + `secciones`, each with its own Guardar; the ficha warns before leaving a tab with unsaved changes). Creating an activity is a modal that only asks the name (`NuevaActividadModal.jsx`, from "Nueva actividad" or the `+` of a cronograma day, which also sets the date) and then opens its ficha on Datos de la actividad; `/actividades/nueva` only redirects to the list. The ficha's side menu: one button per activity everywhere ("Abrir"). The cronograma has an Área filter next to the year: the área is the participants' (activities have none), via `GET /api/actividades/por-area` (counts only, no names); with an área chosen only the activities with people of that área remain, with that área's inscritos/asistentes (budget and spend stay the whole activity's). The detail modal's Resumen starts with "Para qué se hace" (the activity description). `fecha` is the start date; `fechaFin` is optional for multi-day activities (never before `fecha`; equal to it is stored as null) and the cronograma shows them on every day and month they touch. State colors are a fixed traffic-light (`--est-*` tokens, independent of the module theme). Purchases can also be associated **from the activity** (`POST/DELETE /api/actividades/[id]/compras`, `GET /api/actividades/compras-disponibles`; needs only editBienestar, touches only `actividadId`, audited on the purchase via `records.js#asignarActividadACompra`), and "Solicitar compra" sends Compras a request (see Solicitudes de compra). Reading the list only needs authentication (the purchase form needs it); `viewBienestar` gates the page and the detail, `editBienestar` create/edit/delete (default admin + registro). Audited as `entityType: "actividad"`. **Deleting** is a confirmation modal (`EliminarActividadModal.jsx`) opened from the trash button of the Actividades table or «Eliminar actividad» in Datos de la actividad: `GET /api/actividades/[id]/eliminacion` (`resumenEliminacion`) lists what goes with it and blocks (with the reason) a closed activity or one with associated purchases; `deleteActividad` first returns to the bodega what came out of it (all of it if the activity was not executed, only the unused part if it was), discards the pending asociador proposals, then deletes (participants, photos, inventory, notes, form and requests cascade) and audits the snapshot plus a «Detalle» of what was deleted. `scripts/bienestar/importarActividades.mjs <archivo.json> [--confirmar]` loads the plan from the Bienestar Excel base export (idempotent by nombre+fecha).
- **Bienestar: participantes, inventario, formulario público y cierre (`lib/participantesBienestar.js` (`resumenParticipacion`: per area `convocados` = every person of the area's list, also those who attended without registering — participation = asistieron ÷ convocados —, inscritos, asistieron, noAsistieron, sinRegistrar, `motivos` and `lider`; Nova's activity analysis receives that per area and must point out where non-participation or a reason concentrates. Nova's 1-5 score never penalizes missing ratings or an isolated negative comment: only a repeated complaint — at least 3 and a quarter of the comments with an opinion, team notes included — or an average under 3.5 with 5+ ratings), `lib/inventarioBienestar.js`, `lib/bienestarExcel.js`, `app/api/actividades/[id]/{participantes,inventario,formulario}`, `app/api/publico/inscripcion/[token]`, `frontend/components/bienestar/FichaActividad.jsx`, `InscripcionPublica.jsx`)**: the module answers: spend vs. budget, who registered and did not attend (and why), participation %, participation by area, inventory used vs. left over, and a rating when closing. **Bienestar and Fundación are kept apart** (each with its own Resumen/Actividades/Cronograma under `/bienestar` and `/bienestar/fundacion`; `?linea=` on the API; Spend in the module, for both líneas and for Nova, only ever counts purchases ASSOCIATED to an activity of that línea, in the month of the activity; unassociated purchases show no value there). `ParticipanteActividad` (one row per person and activity: inscrito, asistio true/false/null, motivoInasistencia from `MOTIVOS_INASISTENCIA`, calificación 1-5, observación, `datos` JSON with the activity-specific fields — sensitive ones (health, EPS, blood type, emergency contacts, minors…) are only returned to editBienestar —, origen formulario/importacion/manual, autorizaDatos) drives the activity counts (`sincronizarConteos`: attendees are only recalculated once some attendance is recorded). `InventarioActividad` (cantidad, usado, costoUnitario → sobrante and its cost). Closing is not a selectable state but an action ("Cerrar actividad", ficha and edit form, confirmation modal `CerrarActividadModal`): only from ejecutada, requires a 1-5 `calificacion` (+ comentarioCierre, cerradaPor/At) and closes the public form. A closed activity can no longer be modified (the backend rejects edits to it, its participants, inventory, photos, form and associated purchases; a purchase's own Compras data still changes). Public form: per-activity random `formularioToken`, `formularioCampos` from `PLANTILLAS_FORMULARIO` (general/carrera/torneo/familiar/fundación); `/inscripcion/<token>` (frontend, no session) → `/api/publico/inscripcion/<token>` (no session, rate-limited by IP, both middlewares let it through), requires the Ley 1581 data authorization and rejects a duplicate document. Excel import (`bienestarExcel.js`, also used by scripts): columns recognised by header (Forms exports, attendance lists; X/SI/OK = attended, "No asiste…" = not + reason), people matched by document, exact name or `buscarPorNombre` (≥2 shared words, unique candidate). The activity page (`/bienestar[/fundacion]/actividades/[id]`) has Resumen / Participantes (paginated, attendance per person, the three Excel imports, per-person data) / Inventario / Compras / Formulario tabs. The Resumen has the same Mensual/Anual + Año (+ Mes) control as the Compras dashboard (`?mes=`). Migration of the Excel folder: `scripts/bienestar/importarParticipantes.mjs <carpeta> [--confirmar]` (file → activity map inside, then the Fundación base "X" marks); `reconstruirLocal.mjs` cleans and rebuilds it all in the local DB and `generarMigracionCarga.mjs` writes the data migration (loads only if `actividades_bienestar` is empty, never writes the sensitive Excel fields). Audited as `participante` / `inventario` / `formularioActividad` with the activity id as recordId.
- **Bienestar: importar con Nova, análisis, fotos y Fundación**: (1) `lib/importacionNova.js` + `POST /api/actividades/[id]/participantes/importar-nova` (`ImportarNovaModal.jsx`): the Participantes tab has ONE import button (`tipo: "lista"`, "Importar participantes y asistencia"): the person uploads whatever file they have and Nova decides what it is (list of programados, list of those who attended, or a sheet with who came, who did not and why) and which columns are asistencia and motivo; a second call (`interpretarValores`) gets only the DISTINCT values of those two columns (never the rows or names) and says for each one asistió / no asistió / sin dato, the motivo from the `motivoInasistencia` catalog and what an empty cell means, and the code applies that reading to every row (fixed rules `leerAsistencia` as fallback). Mode: only a plain list of attendees is loaded as `asistentes`; anything else as `inscripcion` (people stay programmed, with attendance and motivo where the file says so). The separate types `programados` / `asistentes` (agente de importación) and `calificaciones` (Encuesta y observaciones tab) still exist. Nova only sees a sample and returns the file's structure (sheet, header row, column of each field, rating scale); the code reads every row and applies it through `aplicarParticipantes`. Without `confirmar` it simulates and writes nothing; confirming resends the same mapping, so Nova is not called again. (2) `lib/analisisBienestar.js` + `/api/actividades/analisis` (`AnalisisNova.jsx`, model `AnalisisBienestar`, one per línea/año/mes, mes 0 = general of the year): Nova writes text over the figures of `resumenBienestar`, it never computes them. Generated by itself on the 1st of each month (the month that ended + the general; recovered on startup if the backend was down; `ANALISIS_BIENESTAR_AUTO=0` disables it) and on demand with "Resumir de nuevo" (editBienestar), which replaces it. Audited as `analisisBienestar`. (3) `FotoActividad` (`lib/fotosBienestar.js`, `FotosActividad.jsx`, Fotos tab): resized in the browser, stored as bytes, sha1 dedupe per activity, audited as `fotoActividad`; photos from the monthly PDF reports are migrated with `scripts/bienestar/extraerFotosInformes.py` + `importarFotos.mjs`. (5) `AnalisisActividad` (`lib/analisisActividad.js`, `POST /api/actividades/[id]/analisis`, `CalificacionActividad.jsx`): Nova gives an executed or closed activity its own 1-5 score + analysis **only when new information comes in** (no periodic sweeps): every audited change that counts for the score (activity fields or the switch to ejecutada, participants, inventory, team notes, associated purchases — `audit.js#cambioQueCalifica`) schedules `analisisActividad.js#reanalizarEnSegundoPlano`, which waits 3 minutes after the last change so an import costs one AI call; closing analyses it itself, shown next to the activity in the cronograma; when closing, the person rates it 1-5 and Nova analyses it again (only aggregates and anonymous comments go to the AI; if it fails the closing stands); clicking a rating in the table or the ficha opens `CalificacionModal`. (6) Bienestar catalogs (`lib/catalogosBienestar.js`, module "Bienestar" in `maestros.js`, page `/bienestar/maestros`, also a group in `/maestros`): `responsableBienestar` (users; an activity's responsable must be one), `areaParticipante` (with an optional `lider`, column of `maestros`, migration `lider_area_bienestar`, editable even when the area is in use; shown on hover in the activity's participation by area), `motivoInasistencia`, `unidadInventario` (id + text) and `escalaCalificacion` (5 fixed rows, rename only). Activity states and líneas are NOT catalogs: rules depend on them. Excel imports match catalogs ignoring accents and signs and create missing areas/units; `scripts/bienestar/sincronizarCatalogos.mjs` unifies area spellings and fills ids after a data migration. (7) Cronograma has a third view, Trayectoria (`CronogramaTrayectoria.jsx`); every view opens `DetalleActividadModal` (mini dashboard: budget, attendance, who went, reasons, Nova analysis); Trayectoria has a full-screen toggle. (8) The Nova mascot in the Bienestar module (`NovaBienestar` in `FloatingAgentChat.jsx`, `.nova-bien` in globals.css) uses `public/nova/nova-bienestar-*.png`, without the cart, with a beating heart and a meditation phase. (4) Fundación has its own dashboard (`ResumenFundacion.jsx`, sky-blue colors scoped to `.resumen-fundacion`, logo in `public/bienestar/conectando-corazones.png`): same API figures, told as jornadas and people who accompanied. There is no beneficiary data, so neither the dashboard nor Nova may state how many beneficiaries were served.
- **Inventario (bodega) de Bienestar (`lib/inventarioBodega.js`, `app/api/inventario/*`, `app/api/records/[id]/inventario`, `app/api/actividades/[id]/inventario/bodega`, page `/bienestar/inventario` — menu item "Inventario" —, `frontend/components/bienestar/InventarioBodegaView.jsx`, `SacarDeBodega.jsx`, `components/EntradaBodegaCompra.jsx`, models `ArticuloInventario`/`MovimientoInventario`, migration `inventario_bodega_bienestar`)**: what is in the warehouse for the activities, shared by Bienestar and Fundación. **Its own Matriz features** (Bienestar tab, section Inventario; default admin + registro, migration `permisos_inventario_bienestar` copied the access of custom roles from their Bienestar/Fundación/Compras features): `viewInventarioBienestar` (page, menu, history), `editInventarioBienestar` (articles, entradas, salidas, ajustes), `usarInventarioBienestar` ("Usado de la bodega" in an activity, its pencil and deleting a bodega row — plus editing the activity) and `entradaInventarioCompra` (the RecordModal block, prop `puedeBodega`; default also legalizar). Three bodegas (bodega / sst / aseo, the three tables of the Excel «BIENESTAR GENERAL 2026» › Inventario Bodega, loaded by the migration only if the table is empty: 63 items with their initial/entradas/salidas as movements). Each article is a LOT (same product, another expiry date = another row), with optional `minimo` ("Faltan N") and `vence` (same traffic light as the Excel: 0–30 por vencer, 31–70 alerta, 71+ vigente). **The stock only changes through a `MovimientoInventario`** (inicial / entrada / salida / ajuste / devolucion, signed `cantidad`, `stockAntes`→`stockDespues`, origin `recordId` or `actividadId`) and never goes negative: outputs decrement with a conditional `updateMany(stock >= n)` inside the transaction (409 otherwise). Inputs: by hand (+ in the table) or from a saved purchase (block "¿Esta compra entra a la bodega de Bienestar?" in `RecordModal`, createRecord/editRecord, registered at once as an entrada linked to the purchase). Outputs: by hand, or from an activity (an activity's inventory has ONE number per item, what was used: `cantidad` = `usado`, table columns Ítem / Unidad / Usado / Costo; "Usado de la bodega" in the ficha's Inventario section: the person only types how much was USED, no prior estimate — it is discounted at once and creates/adds to an `InventarioActividad` row with `articuloId` and `cantidad` = `usado`; the row pencil «Editar la cantidad usada» (`ajustarCantidadDeBodega`, accion `cantidad`) discounts or returns the difference; deleting the row returns all of it; its cantidad/usado cannot be edited through the normal PATCH). The table search looks in the three bodegas at once (accent-insensitive, every word). Ajuste = physical count. Audited as `inventarioBodega` (recordId = article id). Mínimos come empty: each person sets them with the pencil. The table has filters by **estado** (disponible / faltan / agotado / inactivo) and **vencimiento** (vencido / por vencer 0–30 / alerta 31–70 / vigente 71+ / sin vencimiento), combined with the KPI cards and the search, and **«Buscar con Nova»** (`POST /api/inventario/nova`): Nova only translates the request into those filters (bodega, estado, vencimiento, words), validated in code; it never sees the articles nor gives figures.
- **Encuesta y observaciones de una actividad (`lib/observacionesActividad.js`, `app/api/actividades/[id]/observaciones`, `bienestar/ObservacionesActividad.jsx`, model `ObservacionActividad`)**: tab of the ficha with "Importar calificaciones" and one paginated table of every rating and comment (team notes, participants, the activity's observaciones and the closing comment); `DetalleActividadModal` shows the same table read-only (`enModal`). Only team notes are created and deleted there (not on a closed activity); they go to Nova as anonymous text and are audited as `observacionActividad`, which the evaluation agent watches.
- **Tono de los comentarios (`lib/sentimientoComentarios.js`, `bienestar/Sentimiento.jsx`)**: Nova marks every Bienestar comment as positivo / neutro / negativo (thumb up, sideways or down) or no_aplica (no opinion about the activity: "N/A" text, no thumb; shown next to the text in the Participantes and Encuesta y observaciones tables and the cronograma modal). Stored next to the text (`ParticipanteActividad.sentimiento`, `ObservacionActividad.sentimiento`, `ActividadBienestar.sentimientoObservaciones`/`sentimientoCierre`); whoever writes a text sets it back to null and calls `clasificarEnSegundoPlano`, and nothing else runs it periodically (only new or edited comments are classified) (`SENTIMIENTO_AUTO=0` disables both). Only saved if the text did not change meanwhile; audited as `sentimientoComentario` (not watched by the evaluation agent). The Bienestar and Fundación dashboards end with the period's top 5 positive and top 5 negative comments (`resumenBienestar().comentariosDestacados`, `ComentariosDestacados.jsx`, no author names); clicking one opens `DetalleActividadModal` on its Comentarios tab.
- **Agente de importación (`lib/agenteImportacion.js`, `lib/archivoSeguro.js`, `POST /api/actividades/[id]/agente-importacion`, `bienestar/AgenteImportacion.jsx`, tab of the ficha for editors of an open activity)**: the user drops every file of the activity at once. `archivoSeguro.js` checks the CONTENT, not the extension: only .xlsx/.xls/.csv pass, rejecting macros, ActiveX, embedded objects/programs, external links, zip bombs and formulas or CSV cells that could run commands; rejections are audited (`agenteImportacion`, action `rechazar`). Photos go through the photos route after the browser re-draws them as JPEG. For each sheet Nova decides the type (programados / asistentes / calificaciones / inventario / ninguno) and the mapping; the code simulates the load (nothing written) and asks the user what only they know (the type when Nova is unsure, whether unlisted people did not attend); "Importar todo" applies each plan with the answers via `importarConNova` / `importarInventario`, without calling Nova again, and audits each file.

- **Agentes de Bienestar (menu "Agentes", page `/bienestar/agentes`, `frontend/components/bienestar/agentes/*`, migration `agentes_compras_bienestar`; for editBienestar/editFundacion)**: two agents (the Cotizador was removed from Bienestar in November 2026; it lives on in Compras › Agentes), none saves anything without a person confirming. (1) **Asociador** (`lib/agenteAsociador.js`, `/api/actividades/agentes/asociador`, model `PropuestaAsociacion`): for each Bienestar-area purchase without activity the code picks candidate activities (date window, shared words, category/supplier of their other purchases) and Nova chooses one with a confidence; the confidence is capped in code (alta only if the purchase names the activity, media needs a concrete signal, date alone = no proposal). "Sí, asociar" goes through `records.js#asignarActividadACompra` (audited on the purchase) and checks the activity line's edit permission; accept/discard are audited as `propuestaAsociacion`, and so is every search run (`logBusquedaAsociador`, action `buscar`). **Agents never run without a person asking**: "Buscar ahora" runs it on demand (no Bandeja notice, results on screen); the daily automatic review only runs if someone turns on its switch (model `ConfigAgente`, `lib/configAgentes.js`, migration `config_agentes`, off by default, audited as `configAgente`), and only that one sends a Bandeja notice. The hourly check in `instrumentation.js` does nothing while the switch is off (`AGENTES_COMPRAS_AUTO=0` disables it entirely). A purchase is not re-checked for 30 days. (2) **Compras de una actividad** (`lib/agenteComprasActividad.js`, `POST /api/actividades/[id]/sugerir-compras`): Nova proposes the list from purchases of similar past activities, citing reference purchase numbers; the estimated value is the average of those references (never AI), total vs budget computed in code; "Crear solicitudes" creates them via `crearSolicitud`. Shortcut from the activity's Solicitudes. (3) **Cotizador** (`lib/agenteCotizador.js`, `/api/actividades/agentes/cotizador[/extraer|/{id}]`, model `CuadroComparativo`): quotes as PDF/Excel/CSV/photo are checked by content (PDFs with JavaScript, launch actions, embedded files or active forms are rejected and audited), Nova extracts each one (`aiStructured` now accepts a PDF `documento`), the code computes totals, differences and the procedure threshold (2/4 SMMLV, min 3 quotes) and Nova writes the recommendation; saved and exported to PDF, audited as `cuadroComparativo`.
- **Historias de Fundación (`lib/historiasFundacion.js`, `GET /api/actividades/historias?anio=`, page `/bienestar/fundacion/historias`, `frontend/components/bienestar/historias/*`)**: the Fundación jornadas already done (ejecutada/cerrada with date up to today) told as a presentation with the Conectando Corazones logo: a page of cards plus the year figures, a full-screen player (`Reproductor.jsx`, a 1280×720 stage scaled to the window; per jornada Portada → Quiénes acompañaron → Galería → Calificaciones y comentarios (participants' average with its 1-5 split or the closing rating, plus only the top 4 positive comments; neutral and negative ones are only counted) → Cierre con Nova (its stars plus an emotional title and closing phrase Nova writes tying the jornada to the program's purpose, shown in full: `AnalisisActividad.tituloHistoria` + `fraseHistoria` + `fraseHistoriaHash`, migration `frase_historias`. **Historias is sentimental: it never shows money (budget, spend, costs, purchases) or inventory** — the technical analysis never reaches the client, its money/inventory sentences are not even sent to the AI, an answer that still mentions them is discarded, and the activity description is filtered the same way; written for all missing jornadas in ONE AI call only when the jornada's data fingerprint changes, saved with raw SQL so the analysis `updatedAt` the evaluation agent relies on does not move), a slide only when there is data for it; "Ver el año completo" starts with an animated year summary and chains them all) and "Descargar PDF del año" (`pdfHistorias.js`, jsPDF, one A4 page per jornada). A third button, "¿Qué es Conectando Corazones?", plays a 7-slide presentation of the program in the same player (`DiaposPrograma.jsx`, text in `historias/programa.js` taken from the program document without the corporate-support/money section or children counts; photos are random from the year jornadas, plus the previous year if there are few, shuffled every time). It reads live data, so it fills itself as activities, attendance, comments, photos and Nova scores are recorded; comments go without author (only positive ones by Nova's thumb), no beneficiary counts. Read-only; gated by viewFundacion.
- **Actividades sin participantes**: `ActividadBienestar.aplicaParticipantes` (check "Aplica participantes y listas de asistencia", paso Asistencia). In false: no participants/form tabs, inscritos and asistentes are returned as null by `listActividades`/`getActividad` so they count in no figure, the public form closes, and Nova neither evaluates nor penalizes attendance. Stored data is kept in case it is reactivated.
- **Agente de evaluación de actividades (`lib/agenteEvaluacion.js`, `app/api/actividades/agente-evaluacion`, `bienestar/AgenteEvaluacion.jsx`, button in Actividades)**: re-scores with Nova only the activities with audit entries (activity data, participants, inventory, purchases; not photos or responsable) after their last score, plus never-scored ones, then refreshes the affected dashboard analyses. The user sees the list and an AI-cost warning first; POST requires `confirmar: true`. One in-memory background job per server, with progress and stop. **Any new Bienestar data that affects the score must be audited, or the agent will not see the change.**
- **Nova en Bienestar**: `resumen_bienestar`, `buscar_actividades` y `detalle_actividad` (`agentTools.js`) validan el permiso de cada línea y solo devuelven cifras agregadas, sin nombres ni datos de participantes. `buscar_actividades` acepta `realizadas` (ejecutadas o cerradas hasta hoy, hora de Colombia) y `desde`/`hasta`, y trae los conteos ya calculados. `fotos_actividad` devuelve un bloque ```` ```fotos {"actividadId":N} ```` que el chat pinta como galería (`FotosEnChat.jsx`, que pide la lista y cada imagen con la sesión de quien mira; el mismo componente es la pestaña Registro fotográfico de `DetalleActividadModal`). Nova sabe qué es Conectando Corazones: `lib/conectandoCorazones.js` es la fuente única (copia legible en `docs/conectando-corazones.md`, actualizar las dos), el chat lleva un resumen + el índice y pide las secciones con `consultar_referencia` documento `"fundacion"`; el análisis de Fundación y las frases de Historias usan su propósito y valores (Historias nunca la parte de apoyo corporativo); sus cifras son de la fundación, nunca beneficiarios atendidos por las jornadas y la moderación (`agentModeration.js`) acepta Bienestar y Fundación como tema. Las sugerencias del chat son preguntas de gerente al azar (`frontend/lib/preguntasGerente.js`). `AgenteChat` y `FloatingAgentChat` cambian texto y sugerencias según el módulo activo (`useModulo`).
- **Presupuesto sin configurar (2026-11-11)**: an activity whose `presupuesto` is empty or 0 takes as its budget the accumulated value of its associated purchases. `toClientActividad` returns `presupuesto` = effective value (configured, or `gastado`), `presupuestoAsignado` = what was typed (the form edits this one, never the effective value) and `presupuestoConfigurado`; `pctPresupuesto` is null when not configured. The ficha shows the «El presupuesto no está configurado» notice in Presupuesto, the table/próximas/calificación show the value with a «sin configurar» tag, and dashboard/trayectoria totals sum only configured budgets (`kpis.presupuestoAsignado`, `gastoSinPresupuesto` by `!presupuestoConfigurado`). Nova receives `presupuestoConfigurado` / `notaPresupuesto`.
- **Rule: not every purchase belongs to an activity.** Neither the dashboards nor Nova warn about or suggest associating área purchases without an activity (the old «Compras del área Bienestar sin actividad» banner and `comprasDelAreaSinActividad` were removed); associating stays available from the activity and the asociador agent.
- **Nova's activity score without participants**: when `aplicaParticipantes` is false, `analisisActividad.js` appends `SIN_PARTICIPANTES` to the system prompt (rubric limited to budget/spend, inventory, closing rating/comment and team notes; attendance is never a missing datum nor a penalty) and drops any «alerta» point about attendance/inscritos.
