# Módulo Tesorería

Detalle del módulo (reconstruido desde cero en diciembre de 2026). Las convenciones generales siguen en `CLAUDE.md`.

- **Tesorería — third module, rebuilt from scratch in December 2026 (`lib/tesoreria/*`, `app/api/tesoreria/*`, pages `/tesoreria[/movimientos|/pagos|/periodicos|/importar|/agentes|/conciliacion|/saldos|/reportes|/maestros]`, `frontend/components/tesoreria/*`, migration `tesoreria_desde_cero` — it DROPPED every earlier Tesorería table and its data; data now only comes in by uploading the Excel template from the app, never by migration; violet theme `data-modulo="tesoreria"`, its modals carry `.tes-tema` because they render in `<body>`)**: replaces the Excel «Flujo de Caja». **Access is by ROLE in the Matriz** (tab Tesorería): `verTesoreria` (consulta) / `registrarTesoreria` (registro: movements, payments, periodic expenses, associations, agent, import) / `firmarTesoreria` (maestros), default admin only; `comun.js#accesoDe(user)` → `{ nivel, puedeRegistrar, puedeFirmar }`, `ruta.js#conTesoreria` wraps every route, `/api/auth/me` returns `tesoreria`. Model (all Int ids, creator columns, protected against U+FFFD): **maestros** `BancoTesoreria` (one per bank tab of the Excel; moneda ISO 4217, formatoExtracto), `MedioPagoTesoreria` (the «Medio de pago» column — AV Alamos, Distrimax, Tarjeta crédito, Efectivo…; INDEPENDENT of the banks), `ItemTesoreria`, `ConceptoTesoreria` (default ítem), `PagadorTesoreria` («Paga»); names that only differ in accents/case are rejected (`maestros.js#claveNombre`). **`MovimientoBanco`** = a real bank movement, one unified form for every bank: tipo debito/credito, fecha, descripción, valor (always positive), concepto (free text, as in the Excel), tercero, observación, referencia and the bank-specific tipoCarga/tipoSolicitud/preparador/formato; soportes (`SoporteMovimiento`, «Leer con Nova» only fills the form). **`PagoProgramado`** = a row of «Detalle 2026»: anio/mes/fecha (`fechaAproximada` when the Excel only had the month), tipo **I ingreso / E egreso / J jornal / S saldo / T traslado** (I, E and J add up in income/expense — J by its sign; S and T are recorded but do not add up; `comun.js#esIngreso/esEgreso`), estado **P** pending / **E** paid (+`estadoPor` asociacion/manual/importacion), valor WITH SIGN like the Excel (expense negative), ítem, concepto, medio, paga, `gastoPeriodicoId` + `periodo` (unique together). **`AsociacionPago`** movement ↔ payment, MANY-TO-MANY, with the amount the person assigns to each payment (`asociaciones.js`): a movement can't split more than its value; a payment with ≥1 association becomes E, and goes back to P when all are removed (only if it became E by association); the payment shows the DIFFERENCE |programado| − asociado and the person fixes the payment's or the movement's value. Associating is optional when registering a movement (`asociaciones` in the POST) and can also be done from the payment (`PUT /pagos/{id}/asociaciones`). **`GastoPeriodico`** (`periodicos.js`): periodicidad quincenal (cut-off day and 15 days later) / mensual / bimestral / trimestral / semestral / anual, día de corte, fechaInicio, optional fechaFin; on create/edit it generates its P payments from the current month (never backwards) to fechaFin or December, the value being the **last real paid value** of that expense (else its estimate); editing redoes only future pending payments without movements; turning it off deletes those; «Replicar pagos» (`replicarAnio`) fills a whole year with the active ones, idempotent. **Import** (`plantillaExcel.js` pure reader + `importarPlantilla.js`, `/api/tesoreria/importar/{analizar,aplicar}`, `ImportarPlantilla.jsx` 4-step wizard): sheets are classified by their headers — bank tabs (fecha + valor + tipo de transacción or FORMATO = sure; only fecha/descripción/valor = doubtful, the PERSON answers «¿es de banco?») and payment tabs (Ítem, INGRESO/EGRESO, ESTADO, Concepto, Año, Mes, Valor; only the biggest one of the most recent year is preselected, e.g. «Detalle1» is another view of the same rows); the person maps each bank tab to a bank of the maestro (or a new one). Sign of a bank row: tipo de transacción, else FORMATO (incremento/rendimientos enter), a negative value with a type is a reversal (opposite direction); SALDO INICIAL/FINAL rows → `SaldoReportadoBanco` (not movements); dates more than a year away from their neighbours are damaged → the month column is used (`fechaAproximada` + alert). Payment rows with «-» load as 0 (alert); S rows are NOT loaded as payments (alert «regístralo en Saldos iniciales»); groups repeated in ≥3 months (tipo+ítem+concepto+medio+paga) are proposed as periodic expenses — the person confirms periodicity and cut-off day and the Excel rows get linked to the new expense. Every row has a `claveImportacion` fingerprint (movements: bank+fecha+tipo+valor+descripción+n-th; payments: año+mes+tipo+ítem+concepto+medio+paga+n-th, WITHOUT state/value) so uploading again never duplicates and a payment that went from P to E in the new Excel is updated if it was still P without movements. Bank extracts (`extractos.js`, PDF/Excel/CSV via `parsersExtracto.js`/`extractoPdf.js`; a full month or a PARTIAL one — yesterday, month to date) create movements with the same fingerprint; a line already loaded from ANOTHER file of the bank (the daily Excel and the monthly PDF describe it differently) is recognised by day + type + value, each existing movement used once; a line equal to a hand-registered movement verifies it. The generic reader takes the sign from a «tipo de movimiento» column («Plata que salió / entró», Débito / Crédito); CSVs are read raw (the xlsx library otherwise shifts «2026-10-31» a day by time zone). The bank balance is stored with its date (`SaldoReportadoBanco.fecha`, migration `tesoreria_saldo_con_fecha`): a partial extract is compared in Conciliación against what was registered up to that date. **Registrar con soporte** (button in Movimientos, `FormMovimientoModal conSoporte`): the person uploads the receipt photo/PDF, Nova reads fecha/valor/tipo/banco/tercero/referencia (`soportes.js#leerSoporte`), and if a movement with the same value and date ±3 days already exists (e.g. from the extract) the form offers «Adjuntar el soporte a este» instead of creating a duplicate. It also lists the **possible payments** of the receipt (`soportes.js#pagosPosibles`, computed by code: payments still without movement, same direction, date ±45 days, value within 15 %; alta = same value and ≤10 days, media = same value farther or ≤2 % near, baja = ≤15 %; shared words with concepto/ítem raise it), colored by confidence like the agents; «Sí, es este pago» puts it in the association so on save (or when attaching to the existing movement that had no payments) the payment is associated and becomes E. **Gastos periódicos**: year filter (`GET /periodicos?anio`, vigentes that year with that year's counts) with `comun.jsx#SelectorAnio` (years with data, two back to three ahead, «Otro año…» to type any, e.g. 2028; also in Pagos programados); «Llenar un año» (`LlenarAnioModal`): replicar from a source year (only the gastos that had payments in it, or all active; `GET /periodicos/replicar` previews how many payments would be created and how many already exist) or proponer con Nova for the whole year, shown one month at a time with the same `tes-mes` chips; a gasto with paid payments or associated movements (any year: `pagadosTotal`/`conMovimientos`) cannot be deleted and the row says why and offers to turn it off. **Agente de asociación** (`agenteAsociacion.js`, page Agentes — same design as the Compras/Bienestar agent pages: ag-hero, ag-selector with «Asociar movimientos» and «Proponer pagos», CabezaAgente, `PropuestaAsociacion.jsx` cards `ag-propuesta conf-<alta|media|baja>` colored by confidence, also used in Conciliación step 3 — model `PropuestaPagoTesoreria`): for movements with value still unassigned (only years that have payments) it scores pending payments AND E payments without movement (the Excel never says which movement paid what) by value, month, IDF-weighted text, bank = medio de pago and learned history; Nova only breaks near ties (≤40 per run); confidence capped in code (alta = exact value + same month + another signal + no tie; media = exact + another signal, or ≤2 % + same month + signal; else baja). **It never associates by itself**: accept/discard per proposal or «Aceptar todas las de confianza alta», audited. **Proponer pagos** (`proponerPagos.js`): for a month or the year, the missing periods of active periodic expenses (code) + what was paid that month the previous year that is not periodic, where Nova only says whether it looks recurring; nothing is created until the person approves each one (values editable). **Saldos** (`saldos.js`): the person types the January opening balance per bank and year (`SaldoInicialBanco`); the report gives each month's balance (opening + credits − debits) next to the bank-reported one and the difference. **Alertas** (`AlertaTesoreria`, `alertas.js`): what didn't add up on import, open until someone marks it reviewed (fixing a 0 value or typing the opening balance closes it). **Tesorería has NO alerts page of its own** (`/tesoreria/alertas` only redirects to `/alertas`): they arrive in Alertas y notificaciones › Mi día, where each row has «Ir» (to the payment, movement, Saldos or Importar) and «Revisada» (`accion: revisarAlertaTesoreria`, only for registrarTesoreria, `POST /api/tesoreria/alertas/revisar`); the sections are `tesVencidos` (P payments past due, live) and `tesAlertas` (`roles: ["*"]` + `verTesoreria`). **Reportes** (`pivote.js`): the Excel pivots «Ejecutado» (Tipo → Ítem → Concepto) and «Por medio de pago» over the payments, monthly, cell drill-down and xlsx export. Nova tools (`novaTesoreria.js`): resumen_tesoreria, buscar_pagos_tesoreria, buscar_movimientos_tesoreria, saldos_bancos_tesoreria — offered only with access, re-checked per call, **never published in the MCP**. Audited (`audit.js#logTesoreria`) as movimientoTesoreria / pagoTesoreria / gastoPeriodicoTesoreria / asociacionTesoreria / propuestaPagoTesoreria / saldoTesoreria / alertaTesoreria / importacionTesoreria / extractoTesoreria / soporteTesoreria / maestroTesoreria. **Conciliación y cierre** (`conciliacion.js`, page `/tesoreria/conciliacion`, `ConciliacionTesoreria.jsx`, migration `tesoreria_conciliacion`): per month and bank, four steps with plain-language questions: 1) does the balance match the bank? opening balance of the month (January opening typed in Saldos + what was registered before) + credits − debits = calculated balance vs the bank final balance (from the uploaded extract — PDF, Excel .xlsx/.xls or CSV, whose opening/final balances are read from the SALDO rows or the saldo column and stored as `SaldoReportadoBanco` + `ExtractoBancario.saldoInicial/saldoFinal` — or the SALDO rows of the template); 2) is everything registered in the bank? `MovimientoBanco.verificadoAt/verificadoPorNombre/extractoId`: movements from the template or an extract are verified on load, a hand-registered one is verified when an extract line matches it (same day, type and value) or when the person confirms «Sí, está en el banco» (audited `conciliacionTesoreria` verificar); 3) does every movement have its payment? movements with unassigned value with the agent proposal inline (accept/discard/associate by hand); 4) close the month (`firmarTesoreria`): blocked while any bank with movements lacks the opening balance or has a difference with the bank balance; missing extract, unverified, unassociated and pending payments are only warnings. `CierreMes` (one per month, all banks) makes the month read-only (`exigirMesAbierto` in movements, supports, associations, verification); it can be reopened (audited `cierreTesoreria` firmar/reabrir).

**Años, exportación y tarjetas (nov. 2026).** Los selectores de año (`SelectorAnio`, `useAniosTesoreria` en `components/tesoreria/comun.jsx`) muestran dos años atrás hasta 2028, los años con pagos o movimientos y los creados a mano (`tesoreria_anios`, `lib/tesoreria/anios.js`, `GET/POST /api/tesoreria/anios`, auditado como `anioTesoreria`). Un año nuevo solo se crea en Gastos periódicos con «Crear otro año» (`CrearAnioModal`: dejarlo en blanco, replicar desde otro año o proponer con Nova). Desactivar un gasto periódico pide confirmación (`GET /api/tesoreria/periodicos/{id}` = vista previa). Al desactivarlo se borran sus pagos en P con fecha desde hoy, en todos los años, menos los que tienen movimientos asociados. La lista tiene las pestañas Todos / Activos / Inactivos. Cada tabla tiene «Exportar a Excel» (`BotonExcel`, con todas las filas del filtro, no solo la página; los montos van como número con su signo). En Pagos programados las tarjetas filtran la tabla por los pagos que forman su cifra (`sentido=egreso|ingreso` en `/api/tesoreria/pagos`). Los botones secundarios de Tesorería usan el color del módulo (`globals.css`, al final).

**Balances anchored to the extracts (`lib/tesoreria/saldos.js#reporteSaldos`).** The typed saldo inicial is only the starting point; if it is missing, the bank's reported December final balance is used. Every balance the bank reports is an anchor (`SaldoReportadoBanco`, written by extracts, partial extracts and the template's SALDO rows):
- a final balance anchors the close of its `fecha`, or of the last day of the month;
- an initial balance anchors the close of the previous day.

The balance on any date = the last anchor on or before it + the net of the movements after it. With no earlier anchor, it is computed backwards from the next one. So uploading a monthly extract re-anchors that month, and the following months start from the bank's figure. Per month the report returns:
- `fuente`: `banco`, `parcial` or `calculado`;
- `reportado` / `reportadoAl`;
- `calculado`: what the registered movements gave, without that anchor;
- `diferencia`;
- `saldoInicialPrevio`: what the previous month left, ignoring the initial balance of this month's extract.

Conciliación starts each month from `saldoInicialPrevio`, so it also follows the extracts.

**Dashboard (`/tesoreria`, `components/tesoreria/ResumenTesoreria.jsx`).** It has the same format as the Compras Dashboard: `dashboard-shell`, Mensual / Anual plus year and month, KPIs with «Explicar» and a row of small KPIs, and cards in `grid-2` with Resumen ejecutivo. The figures come from `resumenTesoreria({ anio, mes, vista })` → `tablero` (`armarTablero` in `lib/tesoreria/resumen.js`). The cards link to Pagos programados with `?anio&mes=todo|N&sentido&item&filtro`, which `PagosTesoreria` reads from the URL.

**Extract in two steps, and the conciliation as an accounting bank reconciliation (no pagos).**

- **Upload (`POST /api/tesoreria/extractos`):** it only analyzes (`analizarExtracto`) and writes nothing.
  - It returns: new lines (the bank has them, the system doesn't), `soloEnSistema` (the system has them, the bank doesn't), confirmed ones, and system balances vs. extract balances.
  - It also returns the reconciliation (`conciliacion`: partidas + unexplained difference + hints).
  - The analysis stays in memory for 30 min under a `token`.
- **Approve (`POST /api/tesoreria/extractos/aplicar` `{ token, aprobados, guardarSaldos }`):**
  - It records only the approved lines.
  - It ties every existing movement the extract confirms (`extractoId`) to that extract.
  - It saves the unapproved lines in `ExtractoBancario.pendientes` (JSON).
  - It saves the balances only if approved.
- **Conciliación does not handle associations with pagos** (that is the agent's job). Step 2 = `diagnosticar` in `lib/tesoreria/conciliacion.js`:
  - What is «in books, not in the bank»: movements on dates an extract covers, with no `extractoId`.
  - What is «in the bank, not in books»: an extract's `pendientes`.
  - The accounting table (`ConciliacionContable.jsx`) and the hints for the unexplained difference (`PistasDiferencia`): same value, wrong type (half), duplicates, swapped digits (multiple of 9), pairs, comes from the previous month.
  - Each hint's action is approved in a confirmation modal: delete / change type / fix value / record (`POST /api/tesoreria/conciliacion/pendiente`).
- **Balances:** `reporteSaldos(anio, { dias })` returns `saldosEn` for specific days.
- **Year lists:** `GET /api/tesoreria/anios` returns `anios` (earlier years only if they have pagos) and `aniosMovimientos` (also years with bank movements; Movimientos, Saldos, Conciliación).

**Agent «Pagos sin movimiento»** (`buscarPorPagos` in `lib/tesoreria/agenteAsociacion.js`; Agentes, turquoise tab).
- **Direction:** the reverse of «Asociar movimientos». It takes pagos that should already be paid: P with a past due date, or E with no movement. Filters: `alcance`, year, month.
- **Matching:** it looks for the bank movement with the same direction and unassigned value, in the pago's month or the months next to it.
- **Scoring:** same as the movimientos agent, plus how close the date is. Nova only breaks ties.
- **Never associates on its own.** Its proposals carry `agente = "pagos"` (`PropuestaPagoTesoreria.agente`, migration `tesoreria_agente_pagos`). Each agent replaces only its own pending proposals. A pair already proposed by the other agent is not repeated. The listing and «aceptar altas» filter by `agente`.
- **Pago form (`CamposPago`):** blocks with questions (type cards → concept → value and due date with quick dates → payment method), plus a live summary of how the payment will end up.

**Nova for the manager (`lib/tesoreria/novaTesoreria.js` + `lib/tesoreria/consultas.js` + `lib/tesoreria/periodos.js`).**
- **Periods:** computed on the server in Colombia time (`rangoTesoreria`: hoy, mañana, esta/próxima semana Monday to Sunday, este mes complete, `pendientes_al_fin_de_mes`, `mes`, rango…). Nova never does date arithmetic.
- **Tools:**
  - `agenda_pagos_tesoreria`: by due date, or by the date it was paid (the date of the associated bank movement). It separates overdue from upcoming.
  - `estado_pago_tesoreria`: «¿ya se pagó X?», with an optional month; includes the movement and its receipts.
  - `reporte_banco_tesoreria`: what went out or came in, by month, «en qué se fue», the largest movements, balances at the start and end, and the months that don't reconcile.
  - `saldos_hoy_tesoreria` and `soporte_pago_tesoreria`: Markdown links to `/api/tesoreria/soportes/{id}`.
- **Balance rule:** a balance is only real if the bank's last closed month has its extract. Otherwise Nova says it can't be known and gives the estimate: last balance ± registered movements.
- **Access:** the tools are only offered with Tesorería access (`accesoDe`) and are checked again on every call. Without access, the prompt lists Tesorería as restricted. The moderator (`agentModeration.js`) recognizes pagos, properties and concepts as Tesorería topics.

**Tesorería notifications (fuente `tesoreria`, permission = `accesoDe`).**
- **Scheduled:** `pagos_tesoreria` with preset `por_pagar` / `pagados` / `pagados_y_por_pagar` / `vencidos`, `PERIODOS_TESORERIA` (complete weeks and months, and forward-looking), item and text. `movimientos_tesoreria` with banco and sentido.
- **Events:** `tesoreria.pago_pagado` (filters item and text) and `tesoreria.movimiento_registrado` (banco, sentido, text). They are published from `lib/tesoreria/eventos.js` after the write, re-reading the data:
  - association with a movement, manual edit, payment created already paid;
  - movement created by hand or with a receipt, approved from an extract, recorded from the conciliation.
- **No events from the template import.**
- **Fix that applies to every scheduled notification:** `ejecutarConsulta` converts the parameters stored as JSON text. Before, they ran with their default values.
